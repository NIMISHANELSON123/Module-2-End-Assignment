# Module-2-End-Assignment (ABC Company Employee Analysis)

## Overview
This project involves analyzing a dataset from ABC Company consisting of 458 rows and 9 columns. The objective is to preprocess the data, perform various exploratory data analyses, and present insights through data visualizations using Python.

## Preprocessing
* Cleaned and corrected the "height" column by replacing values with random numbers between 150 and 180 to ensure data consistency.

## Analysis Tasks & Observations
1. **Team Distribution:** Analyzed employee distribution across teams and calculated percentage splits.
   * *Observation:* The analysis of team-wise employee distribution and percentages reveals that certain teams have a higher concentration of employees, while others have fewer members.
2. **Position-wise Segregation:** Categorized employees based on their positions within the company.
   * *Observation:* Segregating employees by their positions provides clear insights into which designations have the highest staffing levels and which have fewer personnel within the company.
3. **Predominant Age Group:** Identified the main age groups of employees.
   * *Observation:* The age distribution analysis clearly indicates that the majority of the company's employees fall within the 20-30 age group, making it the predominant age bracket.
4. **Salary Expenditure:** Discovered which teams and positions have the highest salary expenditures.
   * *Observation:* Identifying the teams and positions with the highest salary expenditure highlights where the company allocates the majority of its payroll budget.
5. **Correlation (Age vs Salary):** Investigated the relationship between employee age and salary.
   * *Observation:* The scatter plot and correlation value between age and salary show a very weak correlation, indicating that age is not a primary factor determining salary levels in this dataset.
